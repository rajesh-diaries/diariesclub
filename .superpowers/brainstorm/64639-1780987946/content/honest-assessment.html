Honest Technical Assessment

Will the smart fallback actually work in production?

Android — ✅ Yes, rock solid

  • upi://pay?pa=... is an official Android intent scheme
  • System chooser shows real app icons — guaranteed
  • Works on every Android device with a UPI app
  • Used by millions of apps (Swiggy, Zomato, etc.)

iOS Deep Links — ⚠️ Risky / Brittle

Google Pay Was tez://, now gpay://, may change again
PhonePe Scheme changed 2x in past 2 years
Paytm paytmmp:// — most stable, still undocumented
App Store Review Apple has rejected apps for linking to rival payment apps
Return to app After payment, user must manually switch back. No callback.

Reality check: These schemes are undocumented, change without notice, and Apple discourages them. A scheme that works today may break with next week's app update.

iOS QR Code — ✅ Yes, 100% reliable

  • UPI QR is an NPCI standard — every app can scan it
  • No deep links, no URL schemes, no Apple review risk
  • Works today, works next year
  • Same pattern used by petrol pumps, kirana stores, street vendors
  • Users in India are extremely familiar with "scan and pay"

My recommendation

A

Safe: Android intent + iOS QR

Android gets the perfect native chooser. iOS gets a clean QR + copy sheet. Zero fees everywhere. App Store safe. Slightly more friction on iOS but bulletproof.

B

Risky: Try deep links on iOS

Attempt tez:///phonepe:// first, fall back to QR. Better UX when it works, but schemes break and Apple may flag it. You'll need to monitor and update schemes regularly.
