🌿 What is Safari Club?

Safari Club is a recreational morning activity program where your little one explores, plays, and discovers alongside our four safari friends — Gerry the Giraffe, Zena the Zebra, Rafi the Rhino, and Ellie the Elephant.

Each morning is a new adventure through five magical zones. It's not a preschool or daycare — it's a storybook come to life, designed for curious 2 and 3-year-olds.

❓ Questions Parents Ask

Is this a preschool or daycare?
No. Safari Club is a recreational morning activity program. It is not a preschool, daycare, or educational institution.
What should my child bring?
Just a water bottle and a spare set of clothes. We provide all activity materials and a healthy mid-morning snack.
Can I visit during the session?
We have a dedicated parent viewing area. You're also welcome to relax at our cafe with complimentary coffee.
