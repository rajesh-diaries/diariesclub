Delete your Play Diaries account

Play Diaries (developed by Diaries Club) lets you delete your account and associated data from inside the app. If you cannot access the app, you can also request deletion by email.

Option 1 — From inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Play Diaries app on your phone.
  2. Tap your profile icon (top-right of the home screen).
  3. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and personal data are anonymised within 24 hours.

Option 2 — By email

If you no longer have access to the app, email us from the phone number registered with your account:

Email: support@playcafediaries.in
Subject: Delete my Play Diaries account
Body: Your registered phone number (10-digit Indian mobile)

We will verify your identity by SMS and complete deletion within 7 business days.

What gets deleted

The following personal data is permanently removed:

  • Your name, email, and phone number
  • Your children's names, dates of birth, and any uploaded photos
  • Hero progress, adventure tab data, and reflection moments
  • FCM push notification tokens
  • App preferences and settings

What we retain (and why)

For legal and accounting compliance under Indian law (GST, audit), the following anonymised records are kept:

  • Wallet transactions and invoices — retained for 8 years per GST law. Linked only to an anonymised customer ID, not your name or phone.
  • Audit log of major actions (e.g. refunds, staff actions) — for fraud prevention and reconciliation. No personally identifiable information is preserved.

These records cannot be used to identify you after deletion.

Re-creating an account

If you sign up again later with the same phone number, your previous data is not restored — you start with a fresh account.
